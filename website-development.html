Website Development & Maintenance

Scope & Deliverables

What is covered in setup and ongoing maintenance.

Website Design & Architecture

Custom Interface & Content Structure

We plan your page hierarchy, navigation flow, and visual layout around your actual products, services, and inquiry goals.

  • Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
  • Clear typography and visual presentation of real business photography
  • Structured service pages, product catalogues, and project showcases
Development & Deployment

Clean Frontend & CMS Implementation

Whether built as fast static HTML/CSS/JS or on a customizable WordPress foundation, we focus on clean code and reliable operation.

  • Enquiry form configuration, quote request workflows, and WhatsApp/call actions
  • Semantic HTML structure and clean URL architecture ready for search indexing
  • Security hardening, SSL verification, and core asset optimization
Website Improvements

Iterative Page & Section Upgrades

As your business adds new machinery, product lines, or service categories, we expand and refine your existing website.

  • Addition of new service descriptions, case galleries, or catalogue items
  • Refinement of landing layouts and inquiry forms
  • Mobile usability and page-speed improvements
Ongoing Maintenance

Scheduled Monthly Care & Upkeep

Included within the agreed ongoing monthly scope so your website does not fall out of date after launch.

  • Routine content, text, and photograph updates within agreed monthly volume
  • Core software/plugin updates and security checks
  • Form testing, broken link checks, and uptime monitoring
