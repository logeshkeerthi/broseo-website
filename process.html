Stage-by-Stage Detail

What happens at each stage and what you receive.

01 · Discovery and Scope

Before any development or monthly management begins, we examine your current digital setup and agree on the exact boundaries of work.

  • Review of your existing website structure, hosting environment, and mobile usability
  • Assessment of current search engine indexing, local Google Business Profile status, and Instagram account
  • Clear separation of one-time website setup charges from the ongoing monthly scope
  • Written summary of monthly deliverables, timeline, and access requirements
02 · Foundation Setup

We prepare the technical foundation so ongoing monthly updates run on clean, well-structured properties.

  • Website design and development (when a new site or rebuild is commissioned) or baseline technical remediation
  • Core on-page SEO setup, metadata hierarchy, structured schema, and security hardening
  • Google Business Profile verification checks, category alignment, and accurate business attributes
  • Instagram profile structure, bio clarity, highlight design, and content template alignment
03 · Monthly Execution

Once the foundation is live, we execute your agreed recurring scope on a predictable monthly schedule.

  • Website maintenance, security updates, content edits, and agreed feature/page improvements
  • Continuous SEO, AI SEO, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) refinements
  • Regular Google Business Profile updates, photo additions, and service post publishing
  • Monthly Instagram content planning, visual graphic design, caption writing, and scheduled posting
04 · Ongoing Review

At the end of each monthly cycle, we review what was shipped and align on the next cycle's priorities.

  • Clear log of website updates, maintenance tasks, and content published during the month
  • Review of search engine indexing health, AI search entity clarity, and local profile status
  • Alignment on upcoming seasonal services, new products, or business changes for the next month
  • Scope adjustments if your business expands its service requirements
