The Studio & Founders

Hands-on software and search practitioners.

We focus on useful, cleanly engineered digital properties that businesses can actually run and rely on—combining modern development workflows with hands-on technical execution.

Many businesses struggle with outdated websites, unmaintained search metadata, neglected local listings, and inconsistent social profiles because their work is fragmented across temporary freelancers or multi-layered agencies.

BROSEO brings the four daily necessities of a business's public presence into one disciplined workflow: website development and maintenance, SEO/AI SEO/GEO, Google Business Profile management, and Instagram management.

Technical Accountability

From responsive UI/UX and enquiry form reliability to security hardening and search indexing, we maintain the underlying technical health of what we ship.

Structured Monthly Cadence

Work is organized into clear monthly execution cycles so updates, content, and optimizations happen on schedule.

Working Philosophy

How we approach every client engagement.

Scope Clarity

Explicit Separation of Setup and Ongoing Care

We separate one-time website setup and initial technical foundation work from ongoing monthly management. You always know what is being built once and what is being maintained every month.

Connected Channels

Aligned Website, Search, Local, and Social

A service update on your website should also reflect in your search metadata, your Google Business Profile categories, and your Instagram communication. Managing them together prevents misalignment.

Modern Search Readiness

Built for Traditional Engines and AI Answers

Alongside traditional on-page and local SEO, we structure clear entity relationships, factual service descriptions, and clean HTML hierarchy for AI search visibility and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

Transparent Terms

Factual Reporting Without Inflated Promises

We do not sell vague guarantees or unlimited task queues. We agree on a realistic scope starting at ₹6,000/month with a 3-month minimum commitment and report clearly on the work completed.
