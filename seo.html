Dedicated Service Scope

SEO, AI SEO & GEO

Search engine optimization, AI search visibility and Generative Engine Optimization. Structuring your digital presence for both traditional search indices and generative AI answer systems.

Scope & Deliverables

Technical foundations for traditional and generative search.

Traditional Search Engine Optimization

On-Page & Local SEO Architecture

We organize your page titles, headings, URLs, and internal linking around the actual services, products, and geographic locations your customers search for.

  • Keyword and search intent mapping for service and product pages
  • Semantic H1–H4 heading hierarchy, meta titles, and factual descriptions
  • Local search alignment matching your service areas (e.g., regional manufacturing and interior queries)
AI Search Visibility

Entity Clarity & Structured Data

AI search systems rely on unambiguous factual context to identify what a business offers, where it operates, and who it serves.

  • Schema.org JSON-LD markup for organization, local business, services, and FAQs
  • Consistent entity naming, address, and service taxonomy across pages
  • Crawlable, well-formatted text specifications rather than text locked inside images
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Answer-Ready Service Documentation

We write and format page sections so generative engines can accurately extract and cite your business capabilities when answering user prompts.

  • Direct, factual answers to common buyer questions on your service pages
  • Clear specification tables for products, materials, machinery, or commercial terms
  • Elimination of vague filler copy in favour of specific domain terminology
Technical Indexing & Upkeep

Ongoing Search Health Monitoring

Continuous monthly refinement as new pages are added and search indexing evolves.

  • XML sitemap upkeep, robots.txt configuration, and canonical tag hygiene
  • Resolution of crawl errors, broken links, or duplicate metadata
  • Ongoing content additions and page updates within the agreed monthly scope
