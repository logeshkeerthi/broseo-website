Dedicated Service Scope

Google Business Profile Management

Business profile optimization, updates and ongoing management. Keeping your local search and Maps presence accurate, complete, and active.

Scope & Deliverables

Structured management for local search and Maps accuracy.

Profile Optimization

Category, Attribute & Service Alignment

We audit and configure your profile fields so Google accurately matches your listing to relevant local service and product searches.

  • Primary and secondary business category selection
  • Complete service menus, product entries, and factual business descriptions
  • Alignment of Name, Address, Phone, and Website URL with your main website
Regular Profile Updates

Business Posts, Announcements & Hours

An active profile signals to both customers and search systems that your business is operating and attentive.

  • Scheduled Google Business Profile update posts highlighting real services and projects
  • Holiday and special operating hour updates so customers never see outdated hours
  • New product or capability additions as your business offerings change
Photography & Visual Curation

Real Business & Project Imagery

We organize and upload your real business, workshop, interior, or product photographs to represent your work accurately on Google Maps.

  • Formatting and uploading real client project or facility photographs
  • Cover photo and logo verification
  • Zero unrelated stock photography—strictly authentic business visuals
Ongoing Management

Listing Hygiene & Monthly Monitoring

Google frequently applies automated or third-party suggested edits to business profiles; we monitor and maintain your listing's accuracy each month.

  • Monitoring and correcting unauthorized or automated profile edits
  • Q&A section upkeep and review response coordination with your team
  • Monthly status checks included in your Ongoing Review cycle
